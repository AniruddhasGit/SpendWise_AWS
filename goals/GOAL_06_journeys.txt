  Write docs/11 Journeys/ - the end-to-end story of each persona's real working day.
  Journeys exist to find what a per-screen spec misses: the seams between screens.

DELIVERABLES
  1. One journey document per persona. Each walks the persona's real day step by step:
     what they intend, which surface they land on, what they see, what they do, what the
     system does, and what they leave with. Every step cites the P-row that specifies it.
  2. Journeys to cover at minimum:
     USER  - first ever visit and account creation . logging the first expense .
             a normal week of logging . reading the dashboard and changing the period .
             filtering by category from the ribbon . attaching a receipt .
             coming back after a month away . being locked out and getting back in
     ADMIN - finding a user who cannot log in . issuing a reset and handing it over .
             forcing a sign-out . deactivating and reactivating . promoting a second
             admin . reading the audit log after an incident
  3. JOURNEY_INDEX.md - a table of every journey, its persona, the P-rows it traverses,
     and the SEAMS it crosses (where one surface hands off to another).

WHAT THIS GOAL IS ACTUALLY FOR
  Every step where the PRD does not say what happens next is a PRD defect. File it.
  Expect to find: what the dashboard shows on day one with no expenses; what the user
  sees when a receipt upload fails halfway; what happens to a signed-in user the moment
  an admin deactivates them; what the admin sees when the person they reset never uses
  the token. These are seams, and seams are where specs are thin.
