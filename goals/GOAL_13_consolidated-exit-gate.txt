OBJECTIVE

  never new scope. Runs BEFORE any product code.

  Per-goal QA asks "is this artifact right?" Nothing asks "do they agree?" GOAL_02's review
  found 3 contradictions BETWEEN ADRs no single-document check could see. Eight documents
  each derive from the last: a contradiction caught here costs one fix; after the Bible it
  costs re-deriving three documents.

PART 2 - CROSS-ARTIFACT ASSURANCE - one FRESH zero-context agent per lens, in parallel
  X-01 CONTRADICTIONS. Every pair of documents that could disagree -> CONFLICT_MAP.tsv:
       both locations, the incompatible claims, severity, resolved or open.
  X-02 TWO-WAY COVERAGE at every seam: SRC-001<->BRD<->PRD<->Bible<->SIT/DataModel, and
       ADR<->all. Both directions - no-gap AND no-creep. Any non-zero cell is a finding.
  X-03 AUTHORITY AUDIT. Every BUILD_SESSION ADR: does it decide money, secrets, legal,
       product identity, scope, or waive a check? Those belong in Article X. GOAL_02
       shipped two such breaches - assume more.
  X-04 CLAIMED-BUT-NOT-DONE. Every sentence asserting an action ("filed as", "added to")
       verified against the artifact it names. K1 lives or dies here.
  X-05 RED TEAM. What is missing that nobody thought to look for?

PART 3 - THE UNCOACHED VERTICAL SLICE (the only test that actually proves it)
  Hand a FRESH agent the frozen documents and NOTHING else - no conversation, no coaching,
  no answers. Task: build "a user logs an expense and sees it in the dashboard total".
  EVERY question it must ask is a DEFECT IN THE DOCUMENTS, not a failing of the agent.
  Do not answer them. Count them. Fix the specs. Re-run fresh. Iterate until ZERO.
