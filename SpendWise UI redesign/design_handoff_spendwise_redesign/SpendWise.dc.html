Two ways out, and they are not the same. One pauses the account; the other closes it. Neither erases anything.

Your records are kept, not erased. An expense history is the one thing here that cannot be reconstructed, so neither of these throws any of it away and an administrator can undo either one. Take a copy first if you want one — Your data, just above.
